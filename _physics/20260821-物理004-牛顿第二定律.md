# 物理学习 004 - 牛顿第二定律 (Newton's Second Law)

## 概述

**难度**：基础 (Basic)  
**分类**：牛顿运动定律 (Newton's Laws)

掌握力与运动的关系，F=ma是动力学核心公式。

Master the relationship between force and motion. F=ma is the core formula of dynamics.

---

## 核心要点

### 1. 内容：物体的加速度与所受合外力成正比，与质量成反比
Content: Object acceleration is proportional to net force, inversely proportional to mass

### 2. 公式：F = ma
Formula: F = ma

### 3. F是合外力，a是加速度，m是质量
F is net force, a is acceleration, m is mass

### 4. 加速度方向与合外力方向相同
Acceleration direction same as net force direction

### 5. 单位：1N = 1kg·m/s²
Units: 1N = 1kg·m/s²

---

## 重要公式

$$F = ma$

$$a = F/m$

$$m = F/a$
