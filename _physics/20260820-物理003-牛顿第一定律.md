# 物理学习 003 - 牛顿第一定律 (Newton's First Law)

## 概述

**难度**：基础 (Basic)  
**分类**：牛顿运动定律 (Newton's Laws)

理解惯性定律，掌握物体保持运动状态不变的原理。

Understand the law of inertia, master the principle of objects maintaining their state of motion.

---

## 核心要点

### 1. 内容：一切物体总保持匀速直线运动或静止状态，直到外力迫使它改变这种状态
Content: All objects remain in uniform straight-line motion or at rest unless external forces change this state

### 2. 惯性：物体保持原有运动状态的性质
Inertia: property of objects to maintain their original motion state

### 3. 质量是惯性大小的量度
Mass is the measure of inertia

### 4. 力是改变运动状态的原因
Force is the cause of motion state change

### 5. 不受力时，物体保持原有运动状态
Without force, object maintains its original state

---

## 重要公式

$$F = 0 → a = 0 → v = constant$
